The strip under the plate shares its columns. Capital in sums disclosed rounds and commitments for tracked bets up to the selected date; value out sums tracked M&A and licensing headlines, IPO proceeds and upfronts. Both follow the active lens. Bar height scales with the square root of value.

Maturity positions are analyst judgment from public reporting as of 2 October 2026. Each bubble uses the capital measure named in its readout (rounds raised, committed spend, deal headline, cash, revenue or claimed data value), so sizes are comparable in order of magnitude only and are not additive across measures. Headline deal values include contingent milestones; upfront cash is typically a few percent of the headline.

Dates marked approximate are estimated to the quarter. Beyond October 2026, the stated-milestones layer plots company or investor timelines, not forecasts. Lila’s Series B is reported, not confirmed; Tahoe’s valuation dates from 2025; Benchling figures conflict across sources.

Sources include company releases and filings (SEC, HKEX), PitchBook’s Q2 2026 biopharma report, IQVIA’s mid‑2026 M&A update, BioPharma Dive, BioSpace, Fierce Biotech, Endpoints, STAT, Bloomberg, Reuters, The Information and Newcomer.
